Places Visited

Rajasthan

Jaipur, Jodhpur, Ajmer, Pushkar, Udaipur and Jaisalmer.
Highlights: Hawa Mahal, Amer Fort, City Palace, Mehrangarh Fort, Pushkar Lake, Pichola Lake, Jaisalmer Fort and Kuldhara.

Punjab

Amritsar, Jalandhar, Gurdaspur, Attari-Wagah Border and Chandigarh.
Highlights: Golden Temple, Jallianwala Bagh, Partition Museum, Gobindgarh Fort, Devi Talab Mandir, Rock Garden and Sukhna Lake.

Himachal Pradesh

Shimla, Kufri, Kasauli, Kangra, Kullu-Manali, Dharamshala and Dalhousie.
Highlights: Yulla Kanda, Rohtang Pass, Solang Valley, Hidimba Temple, McLeod Ganj, Kangra Fort and Khajjiar.

Delhi & Agra

Delhi, Agra and Mathura-Vrindavan.
Highlights: India Gate, Qutub Minar, Red Fort, Akshardham, Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Sri Krishna Janmabhoomi.

Jammu & Kashmir

Jammu, Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg.
Highlights: Vaishno Devi, Dal Lake, Shankaracharya Hill, Gulmarg Gondola, Baisaran Valley and Betaab Valley.

Ladakh

Leh, Pangong Lake and Nubra Valley.
Highlights: Shanti Stupa, Leh Palace, Hall of Fame, Khardung La, Hemis Monastery, Thiksey Monastery and the Indus-Zanskar confluence.

Uttar Pradesh

Prayagraj, Varanasi, Sarnath and Noida.
Highlights: Triveni Sangam, Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Ganga Aarti, Assi Ghat, Sarnath Buddhist sites and Botanical Garden.

Uttarakhand

Rishikesh, Haridwar, Kedarnath and Chopta-Tunganath.
Highlights: Ram Jhula, Ganga Aarti, Har Ki Pauri, Kedarnath Temple, Gaurikund, Sonprayag and Chandrashila Peak.

Karnataka

Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hampi, Coorg, Udupi, Gokarna and Chikkamagaluru.
Highlights: Bangalore Palace, Lalbagh, Nandi Hills, Mysore Palace, Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat, Jog Falls and Mullayanagiri.

Mumbai

Mumbai, Nashik and Kolhapur.
Highlights: Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Elephanta Caves, Siddhivinayak Temple, Shirdi Sai Baba Temple and Mahalakshmi Temple.

Goa

North Goa and South Goa.
Highlights: Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, Aguada Fort, Basilica of Bom Jesus, Palolem, Agonda and Butterfly Beach.

Tamil Nadu

Madurai, Yercaud, Kodaikanal, Ooty, Coonoor and Vellore.
Highlights: Meenakshi Temple, Yercaud Lake, Lady's Seat, Coaker's Walk, Kodai Lake, Doddabetta Peak and Vellore Golden Temple.

Andhra Pradesh

Lepakshi, Tirupati, Gandikota, Horsley Hills and Mantralayam.
Highlights: Veerabhadra Temple, Tirupati Balaji, Alipiri Mettu, Belum Caves, Gandikota Gorge, Horsley Hill Zoo and Raghavendra Swamy sites.

Telangana

Hyderabad (Charminar, Makkah Masjid, Golconda Fort, Qutb Shahi Tombs)
Hussain Sagar (Buddha Statue, Tank Bund, boating)
Birla Temple, HITEC City, Banjara Hills
Ramoji Film City
Yadadri (Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Temple), Bhuvanagiri and Swarnagiri.

Kerala

Trivandrum, Varkala and Wayanad.
Highlights: Padmanabhaswamy Temple, Varkala Cliff, Chembra Peak, Edakkal Caves, Banasura Sagar Dam and Jatayu Earth Center.

Andaman & Nicobar

Port Blair, Ross Island, Havelock Island and Neil Island.
Highlights: Cellular Jail, Wandoor Beach, Ross Island ruins, Elephant Beach, scuba diving, Natural Coral Bridge and Bharatpur Beach.

Assam - Meghalaya

Guwahati, Shillong, Jowai, Dawki, Mawlynnong, Nongriat and Cherrapunji.
Highlights: Kamakhya Temple, Umiam Lake, Laitlum Canyon, Umngot River, Mawlynnong, Double Decker Living Root Bridge, Nohkalikai Falls and Mawsmai Cave.
